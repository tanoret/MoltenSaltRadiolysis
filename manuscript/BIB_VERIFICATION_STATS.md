### Vollmer2009 — cannot be located as cited

The entry cites `Vollmer, Sebastian — A modular Bayesian approach for computer model calibration — Univ. of Warwick Dept. of Statistics tech. report, 2009`. No such report appears in the WRAP (Warwick Research Archive Portal) catalogue, on Sebastian Vollmer's CV, or via Google Scholar. Vollmer's MSc at Warwick was 2009–2010 and his PhD thesis (2013) was on "Efficient MCMC and posterior consistency for Bayesian inverse problems" — not modular calibration.

The intended citation is almost certainly the canonical "modular Bayes" computer-model-calibration paper:
**M. J. Bayarri, J. O. Berger, F. Liu, "Modularization in Bayesian analysis, with emphasis on analysis of computer models," Bayesian Analysis 4(1):119–150, 2009, DOI 10.1214/09-BA404.**

- Source: https://projecteuclid.org/journals/bayesian-analysis/volume-4/issue-1/Modularization-in-Bayesian-analysis-with-emphasis-on-analysis-of-computer/10.1214/09-BA404.full
