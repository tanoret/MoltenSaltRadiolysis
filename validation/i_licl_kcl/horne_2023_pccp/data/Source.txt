Conrad, J. K.; Cook, A. R.; ... (et al.)
"Impact of iodide ions on the speciation of radiolytic transients in molten LiCl-KCl eutectic salt mixtures."
Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 2023. DOI: 10.1039/D3CP01477K
OSTI preprint: BNL-224523-2023-JAAM (https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1985791)

Data extracted from the OSTI preprint PDF (pdftotext + manual review).

Experimental setup: BNL LEAF facility, 8.7 MeV electron pulses, dosimetry via standard solution
at 6-9 Gy water-equivalent per pulse. KI in LiCl-KCl eutectic + 2 wt% ZnCl2 secondary samples.

Transient species identified:
- e_s- in neat LiCl-KCl: visible to near-IR absorption (eutectic salt)
- Cl2•- (340 nm): expected, observed
- ICl•- (400 nm, decay studied at 671 nm): mixed-halide radical anion, NEW species observed at 1-10 wt% KI
- I2•-: not significantly observed
- Cl3- product band ~220 nm: present in deconvolution

Key finding: I- ions do NOT directly scavenge e_s- significantly relative to inherent base-salt
impurities. The new ICl•- species forms from Cl2•- + I- and decays via 2nd-order kinetics with
Ea = 23.4 ± 5.7 kJ/mol (similar to Cl2•- disproportionation Ea = 24-26 kJ/mol in LiCl-KCl).
