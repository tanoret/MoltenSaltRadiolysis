                                                                                   3.5. Reactions between AgCl and Li/LiCl. Alkali metals
                                                                                are strong reducing agents and have been used to prepare very
                                                                                reactive metal particles suspended in organic solvents.28 We
                                                                                hope that a similar reduction might produce interesting metal
                                                                                nanoparticle catalysts or exotic metal ions in the molten salts.
                                                                                Here, we report the reduction of Ag+ ions by solvated electrons
                                                                                in molten LiCl. Four cases are investigated: (1) An AgCl
                                                                                molecule is added to the 45LiCl melt to test whether it is
                                                                                dissolved and ionized. (2) An AgCl molecule is added to 2Li+
                                                                                + 2e− + 45LiCl to study what happens when there are more
                                                                                solvated electrons than Ag ions, and we ﬁnd that an Ag− ion is
                                                                                formed. (3) Two AgCl molecules are added to 2Li+ + 2e− +
                                                                                45LiCl, resulting in one e− per Ag+ ion and the formation of an
                                                                                Ag2 species. (4) We calculate whether the Ag2 species formed
                                                                                in step (3) would be more stable than the two atoms in bulk
                                                                                solid Ag; we ﬁnd that energy favors the formation of solid Ag.
                                                                                In all these calculations, the energy of two isolated AgCl
                                                                                molecules in the gas phase is used as reference. The energies of
                                                                                these processes are given in Table 1 and in Figure 4a.
